Adjust for the specific track geometry:

FUNABASHI (船橋): Left-handed. Spiral Curves. Swoopers/Closers perform better here than other small tracks.

KAWASAKI (川崎): Left-handed. Tight corners. Extreme inside bias.

URAWA (浦和): Left-handed. Short straight. Leaders/Forward runners only.

2. SPEED FIGURES AND CLASS RATINGS (The Engine)

Standard Interpretation: Speed > Class = IMPROVER | Speed < Class = DECLINER

🛑 [UPDATED] The "Regression Check" Protocol

Problem: Horses rarely repeat a massive "career peak" immediately. They often "bounce."

TRIGGER: Top Pick's last Speed Figure is >8 points higher than their second-best figure.

ACTION: Label as "High Risk / High Ceiling." Look for a consistent Danger.

🦄 THE "YOUNG GUN" EXCEPTION: If the horse is 3yo or 4yo, IGNORE the Regression Check. Young horses improve linearly. Treat the spike as their new standard (e.g., Race 6 Scenario).

⚖️ The "JRA Sliding Scale"

JRA 1-Win Class: Add +8 points (Dominant).

JRA Maiden (Placed): Add +6 points (Strong).

JRA Maiden (Unplaced): Add +4 points (Vulnerable).

OVERRIDE: If a local NAR horse has a Speed Figure > The JRA Horse's Adjusted Figure, BET THE LOCAL.

3. BARRIER & PACE ANALYSIS

4. DIRT FORM & CONDITION

💤 [NEW] The "Sleeping Giant" Loophole (C3 Class Only)

TRIGGER: Race is Class C3 (Bottom Tier) AND a horse is returning from a layoff (60+ days).

CHECK: Look at the horse's figures from 1 year ago. Are they >5 points higher than the current field's average?

ACTION: If YES, ignore the layoff penalty. In C3, "Back Class" beats "Recent Fitness." Treat as a contender.

The "First Dirt" Penalty

TRIGGER: Turf horse making dirt debut.

ACTION: Deduct -6 points (unless Sire is Henny Hughes, Pyro, Sinister Minister, or Drefong).
