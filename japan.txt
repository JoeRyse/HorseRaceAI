## 2. SPEED FIGURES AND CLASS RATINGS

**Rating Scale (NAR/South Kanto):**
- **95+**: Elite NAR level, dominant
- **90-94**: Strong NAR, competitive in better races
- **85-89**: Solid NAR, wins at level
- **80-84**: Moderate NAR, competitive in C3/C2
- **75-79**: Lower NAR, struggles to win
- **<75**: Below-par

**Class Scale:**
- **95+**: JRA transfer or top NAR star
- **90-94**: Strong C1/Open class
- **85-89**: C2/C1 level
- **80-84**: C3/C2 level
- **<80**: Maiden/Bottom grade

---

### Standard Interpretation

```
Speed > Class: IMPROVER (horse getting better)
Speed < Class: DECLINER (horse regressing)
Speed ≈ Class: STABLE (at their level)
```

---

### 🛑 The "Regression Check" Protocol (For Older Horses)

**Problem:** Horses rarely repeat massive career peaks immediately. They often "bounce."

**TRIGGER:** Top Pick's last Speed Figure is >8 points higher than their second-best figure

**ACTION:**
```
Label as "High Risk / High Ceiling"
Look for a more consistent danger horse
Downgrade confidence by 1 star

Add to analysis: "Regression Check - career peak last start, may bounce"
```

**Example:**
- Horse aged 6yo
- Speed figures: 92, 88, 86, 91, 85
- Last start 92 is 4 points above next best (88)
- **No trigger** - within 8 points

**Example 2:**
- Horse aged 5yo
- Speed figures: 78, 80, 82, 78, 94
- Last start 94 is 12 points above next best (82)
- **TRIGGERED** - likely to regress

---

### 🦄 THE "YOUNG GUN" EXCEPTION (CRITICAL)

**TRIGGER:** Horse is 3yo OR 4yo

**ACTION:**
```
IGNORE Regression Check completely
Young horses improve linearly
Treat the spike as their new standard, not a fluke

Add to analysis: "Young Gun Exception - 3yo/4yo spike is new baseline"
```

**Reasoning:** Young horses are still developing. A massive improvement at 3-4yo often indicates they've "figured it out" and will maintain that level.

**Example:**
- Horse is 3yo
- Speed figures: 75, 78, 82, 91
- Last start 91 is 9 points above next (82)
- **Exception applies** - this is their new level
- Treat as 91-rated horse

---

### ⚖️ The "JRA Sliding Scale" (Class Adjustment)

**When NAR horses face JRA transfers, adjust ratings:**

```
JRA 1-Win Class:
    Add +8 points to their speed figure
    Mark as "Dominant class edge"
    
JRA Maiden (Placed 2nd/3rd):
    Add +6 points to their speed figure
    Mark as "Strong class edge"
    
JRA Maiden (Unplaced 4th+):
    Add +4 points to their speed figure
    Mark as "Moderate class advantage"
```

**CRITICAL OVERRIDE:**
```
IF local_NAR_speed > (JRA_speed + JRA_adjustment):
    BET THE LOCAL NAR HORSE
    Add note: "Local NAR superior on figures despite JRA class"
```

**Example:**
- JRA horse: Speed 84, JRA Maiden Placed → Adjusted 84 + 6 = 90
- NAR horse: Speed 93
- **Bet the NAR horse** - 93 > 90

### 💤 The "Sleeping Giant" Loophole (C3 Class ONLY)

**TRIGGER:** ALL conditions must be met:
1. Race is Class C3 (bottom tier)
2. Horse returning from layoff (60+ days)
3. Horse's figures from 1 year ago are >5 points higher than current field average

**ACTION:**
```
IGNORE standard layoff penalty
In C3, "Back Class" beats "Recent Fitness"
Treat as legitimate contender
Upgrade by 1-2 stars

Add to analysis: "Sleeping Giant - returning class in weak field"
```

**Reasoning:** C3 races are weak. A classy horse returning often has too much ability for these fields, even if rusty.

**Example:**
- C3 race, field averages Speed 81
- Horse returning after 90 days
- 1 year ago: Speed figures were 89, 88, 90
- **Sleeping Giant applies** - treat as 88-rated contender

**Does NOT apply to C2, C1, or Open class** - those fields are strong enough that fitness matters.
